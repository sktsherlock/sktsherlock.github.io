## Typography and layout

The refinement follows the restrained hierarchy and whitespace of the user's [Apple reference](https://getdesign.md/apple/design-md). The academic masthead retains Newsreader; other headings and reading text use the platform's sans-serif stack (`-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `PingFang SC`, `Microsoft YaHei`). No Apple font files are redistributed.

- Main section headings: 28 px desktop, 25 px mobile, weight 600.
- Main text and award names: 16 px; secondary descriptions: 14–15 px.
- Publication and institution titles: 20–21 px, weight 600.
- Honor names and years occupy separate columns, stacking on very narrow screens.
- Education uses three columns and research experience uses two; both become single columns on mobile.
