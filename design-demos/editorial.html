About me

Central South University
Graduating in June 2027

My name is Hao Yan (颜浩), and I also go by Sherirto. I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Central South University, under the supervision of Prof. Senzhang Wang. I expect to graduate in June 2027.

From September 2025 to August 2026, I participated in the Joint Ph.D. Supervision Scheme at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University as a fully funded Research Assistant, supervised by Prof. Chengqi Zhang. I also receive research supervision from Prof. Shirui Pan.

I received my B.Sc. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Hefei University of Technology in 2021. Previously, I was a research intern in the Social Computing Group at Microsoft Research Asia (July 2022–January 2023), mentored by Chaozhuo Li, and a remote research intern at Microsoft STCA (February 2023–January 2024), mentored by Weihao Han.

My research interests include:

  • Graph representation learning and graph–language models
  • Multimodal learning and robustness
  • Large language models, agents, and LLM reasoning
  • Looped Transformers and iterative computation

I am extending my research toward LLM reasoning, agent systems, and looped Transformer architectures.

Open to opportunities. I am seeking suitable Research Assistant and Algorithm Engineer positions, with interests in LLMs, agents, and reasoning. I expect to graduate in June 2027.
